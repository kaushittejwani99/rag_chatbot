# Northwind Tyres: Warranty and Returns FAQ

(Fictional sample document for trying out the chatbot.)

## Warranty period

Every Northwind passenger-car tyre carries a 5-year manufacturing-defect warranty from the date of purchase. Two-wheeler tyres carry a 3-year warranty. The warranty ends early if the tread depth falls below 1.6 mm.

## What the warranty covers

The warranty covers manufacturing defects such as tread separation, sidewall bubbles that appear without impact damage, and bead failures. It does not cover punctures, cuts from road hazards, damage from running the tyre under-inflated, or uneven wear caused by wheel misalignment.

## How to raise a claim

Take the tyre and the original invoice to any authorised Northwind dealer. The dealer inspects the tyre and submits the claim through the dealer portal. Claims are reviewed within 7 working days. You get an SMS when the claim is approved or rejected.

## Pro-rata replacement

If a claim is approved in the first 12 months, the tyre is replaced free of charge. After 12 months the customer pays a share of the new tyre's price in proportion to the tread already used. For example, a tyre with 40% of its tread worn costs the customer 40% of the current list price.

## Returns

Unused tyres can be returned within 15 days of purchase for a full refund, provided they have not been fitted to a wheel. Refunds are credited to the original payment method within 5 working days.

## Contact

Support is available Monday to Saturday, 9 am to 6 pm IST, at support@northwind-tyres.example or on the toll-free number printed on the invoice.
